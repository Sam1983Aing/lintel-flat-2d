## Overall

The page is better than it was this morning and the number does not show it, because one item rose and one fell. What rose is earned. The ending's seam and its trees were traced to causes and fixed, and the new opening is the best single addition since the scan: it is what the client asked for, it uses a piece that was already built, and it works on a phone.
