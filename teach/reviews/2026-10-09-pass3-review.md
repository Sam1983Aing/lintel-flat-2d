# Lintel · Casa Serra. Outside review of pass 3

Date: 9 October 2026
Reviewer: outside creative director, same as rounds 1 and 2
Material: the 35 stills in `teach/shots/2026-10-09-after-final/`, set against the round 2 stills (same file names), the round 1 stills and the BEFORE set. I looked at every new still first, then put each one next to its round 2 twin, and for the details that matter I enlarged crops of both. I also ran a plain pixel difference between the two sets to see where anything moved at all. I checked the builder's list of changes in the stills.

Scoring is unchanged. 8 means a paying client who asked for premium takes that part with no notes. Quarter points are used where a part moved and still carries notes. Flicker cannot be judged from stills. For item 1 I was given the measured numbers and I use those.

Short version: three items moved up (bedroom, bath, garden), four did not move at all (plan, patio scan, gallery, ending). No item reaches 8. Three are a quarter point short. The bath mirror, which did not hold last time, holds now. Two of the builder's claims hold only in part (the horizon comb, the three titles). The frame I called the weakest last round, `29-top-built`, is the same picture.

---

## 1. Plan lines and the opening

**Round 1: 7. Round 2: 7.25. Round 3: 7.25.**

Something was done here, and it is not enough to move the score. In `02-lens1` the glazing along the north wall is now one firm line. In round 2 it was a hairline with a grey wedge of hatch beside it. That is a small, clean gain. In `04-lens3` a few of the far strokes along the top left of the horizon are gone. The row of small boxes at the top right is still there and the paving lines across the middle of the frame are the same as before.

The fade also took things it should not have. In round 2 four mullions stood on the blank paper above the horizon in the middle of `04-lens3` as solid strokes. Now one is a hollow outline and the others are stubs, while the diagonal glass hatch they belong to is still drawn. In `03-lens2` the study mullions were a clean double line. Now each is a thin dotted chain. Lines that are half there are what the client meant by "some lines are thinner".

The hairlines I named in round 1 are untouched. In `03-lens2` the entry path below the door and the edges of the garage still break into rows of dots, pixel for pixel as in round 2. `00-hero` and `01-scroll1` are the same pictures as round 2.

The measured flicker in the middle of the opening is unchanged: 2.4 at 120 vh and 3.0 at 170 vh, where the calm first scroll reads 0.51. That stretch is what the client called blinking. The journal quotes him after this pass still seeing "some blinking effects sometimes". The score stays until that number comes down.

Remaining fixes:

1. Bring the middle down toward 0.51. Give the hairlines on the entry path and the garage edges in `03-lens2` a minimum width of about 1.25 px. This was my first fix in round 1 and it has not been tried in three rounds.
2. Fade whole elements, never parts of one. A mullion fades together with its glass or it stays solid. No hollow strokes (`04-lens3`), no dotted chains (`03-lens2`). The row of small boxes at the top right of `04-lens3` should go with the rest.
3. `01-scroll1`: the room counter still sits on "8.00" and "3.00". The logo still sits right above "LINTEL · CASA SERRA", so the name reads twice.
4. The garage walls are still uncapped and dotted in `03-lens2`.
5. Small: both GUEST SUITE labels still touch their bed symbols in `00-hero`.

## 2. The scan follows the camera (patio)

**Round 1: 7.5. Round 2: 7.75. Round 3: 7.75.**

Nothing that bears on this item changed. The pixel difference between rounds 2 and 3 for `14-slide`, `15-patio` and `16-olive` lights up in the olive's leaves and nowhere else. The crown is a little darker. Everything I listed last time is still in the stills, so the score stays where it was. This is the item closest to 8 and the one the client described as what makes the effect premium, so it is a pity to leave it a quarter point short.

Remaining fixes (the same four):

1. Scan by distance. In `14-slide` the loungers and the umbrella are still paper while the lawn, the stone wall and the cypresses behind them are real. The umbrella is still a white shape on a stick against the sky.
2. In the same still "24." is still chopped by the dark wall and "18.00" still hangs in the air at the far end of the court. Fade a figure when the camera starts toward it.
3. `15-patio`: the bronze mullions and the concrete band of the gallery wing are still finished around paper panels.
4. Nav and room counter are close to invisible on the dark paving in `14-slide`.

## 3. The gallery

**Round 1: 6.5. Round 2: 7.5. Round 3: 7.5.**

One fix was tried and it moved the fault from one place to another. The title in `18-gallery` left the track light and went back to the top left. It is easier to read there than in round 2. It is also back on the soft first artwork with its rule line running across the frame, which was my note in round 1. It sits directly under the logo, so LINTEL, ROOM 05 and THE GLASS GALLERY stack up in one corner. The subline runs into the black frame of the second artwork and loses the I of FILE.

One side gain from the glass change: in `20-galleryEnd` the sky through the right-hand glass is a clear dusk blue where it was a beige haze. The first artwork is as soft as before. The scan front on the ceiling in `19-galleryMid` still moves in square blocks. I count about as many large white dots as last time, though particles move between stills so I will not call that either way.

Remaining fixes:

1. The title has now been in two places and both were on something. Start the stop a metre further in so the first artwork leaves the frame, or park the title on the lit wall between the second artwork and the bench. Keep it clear of the logo.
2. The first artwork is still soft and fills a fifth of `18-gallery`. Moving the stop in cures this and fix 1 together.
3. The white dots in `19-galleryMid` and `20-galleryEnd` still read as snow indoors. Smaller, fewer, warmer.
4. The scan front on the ceiling in `19-galleryMid` still leaves a stepped edge.

## 4. The master bedroom

**Round 1: 6.5. Round 2: 7. Round 3: 7.5.**

The best gain here comes from the window. In `22-bedroom` the view is clear again: a dusk sky, the umbrella, the loungers and the pool all read crisp, and the room gets its depth back. The milky glass from round 2 is gone. The soft goods moved a little. In an enlarged crop the top sheet now has a rolled fold with real thickness and the top of the throw has soft waves. The lamp shades are cream with a faint gradient and no longer white cut-outs.

What did not move: the part of the throw that drops over the side is still a flat card with a ruler-straight hem. The duvet is still a box with a sharp vertical corner at the foot. The pillows are the same. The ceramic lamp bases still show their facets and there is no pool of light on the leather or on the nightstand. `21-bedIn` is the same picture as in round 2 and round 1: pillows show through the duvet, the bench is a glossy white capsule and a chopped "9." hangs on the wall.

Remaining fixes:

1. `21-bedIn`: the bed is still see-through mid-scan. Third round. The client's word was "floating" and this is where a visitor still sees it. Keep every object opaque, ink on paper or finished.
2. The bed still reads as a box. Round the foot corner of the duvet, let it overhang the base by a few centimetres, and give the drop of the throw some sag and two or three creases.
3. Lamps. A pool of warm light on the leather and on the nightstand top, and smooth the ceramic bases.
4. `22-bedroom`: the OPEN FILE link still runs into the progress ruler. Nav and counter are very faint on the rug.

## 5. The bath

**Round 1: 7. Round 2: 7.5. Round 3: 7.75.**

The mirror works from where the visitor stands. In `26-bath-end` it shows the walnut slat wall, the window frames and the sky, with real contrast. In `24-bath-start` the reflection even carries the orange scan front across the reflected window, which is a nice detail. No tub in it, which I had asked for. From this angle I accept that. The garden through the glass is crisp again: the olive is dark against a dusk sky where it was a pale ghost. The "21.00" that hung in the window in `25-bath-mid` is gone.

It stops a quarter point short because of small faults that a client would still point at. The red line down the left pier is in `26-bath-end` for the third round. The pink fringe on the right sconce and the ragged right edge of the mirror are still there. The reflection is coarse: the tops of the slats step like a staircase and the reflected plant on the left is built of blocks.

Remaining fixes:

1. The red line down the pier and the pink fringe on the right sconce in `26-bath-end`.
2. The mirror's picture is too small for a mirror this wide. Double its resolution or soften it slightly so the slat tops and the plant stop stepping. Clean the right edge of the frame.
3. `24-bath-start`: the plant is still half transparent. The leaves in front of the paper window are tan, the ones in front of the wall are green.
4. With the glass clear, the white specks now sit on a dark sky in `26-bath-end` and read as stars. Thin them near glass.
5. Check the colour of the reflected window frames. They are rust orange in the mirror and near black in the room.

## 6. The private garden

**Round 1: 6.5. Round 2: 7.5. Round 3: 7.75.**

The fire burns. `27-garden` has a small cluster of flames over the embers, yellow at the base and orange at the tips, and the frame finally has the warm point it was composed around. The olive trunk is dark and rough where it was pale tan. The stray paving slab under the scroll hint is gone. The lavender is spikier and less like purple balls. The shade on the lawn on the right is dappled now and less of a black slab.

It is not an 8 because the fire is drawn and not yet lit. The flames are hard-edged cones, like folded paper, and they throw no light: the rim of the bowl, the two chairs and the grass are exactly as cool as before. The bottom left corner still swallows the nav. WORK and half of STUDIO survive, which reads as a bug.

Remaining fixes:

1. Let the fire light things. Warm light on the bowl's rim, on the inner faces of the two chairs and on the grass in a circle of about two metres. Then soften the flames: more of them, smaller, with the tips fading out.
2. The nav in `27-garden`. Show all of it in paper white or hide all of it. Half a nav is worse than none.
3. The clipped ball under the progress ruler is still a perfect sphere in camouflage print. The scrub on the ridge is still evenly spaced blobs.
4. The sky is a shade bluer at the top and still a flat haze toward the horizon. It wants a dusk gradient.

## 7. The ending

One thing changed. In `30-roll-rest` the logo is dark on the paper where it was white on white, and the nav on the paper side reads. Remaining fixes:

2. The tube still has no paper edge or thickness where the sheet leaves it, the print on it is a faint grid and not the plan, and both ends are out of frame, so there is no spiral to see.
3. `28-lift`: the meadow olives. `34-finale`: the headline still crosses the living room walls of the plan.
4. Logo and nav show on the paper in `30-roll-rest` and are gone in `31-roll-1`. Check in motion that they do not pop on and off within a second.

## Overall

Round 3 was a small round and it went where the work was easiest to see. The mirror is the best fix of the day: it was claimed in round 2, it did not hold, the builder found out why and now it holds from the stop. Clearing the glass lifted the bedroom and the bath together. The fire gave the garden its point. Against that, four of the seven items did not move, and of the six things I asked for at the end of round 2, two were done (mirror, fire), one was half done (bedding and lamps) and three did not land. The middle of the opening was worked on and the number did not move. The top view before the roll was not touched. The titles were moved again and sit on objects again. The page now averages 7.5. What stands between it and 8 is short and specific: the number in the middle of the opening, one murky frame before the roll, a see-through bed that has survived three rounds, loungers that scan after the lawn behind them, and a handful of one-line faults in the bath and the garden. The two items with the largest gaps, the ending and the plan, are also the two that received the least work.

## Claims that did not hold, and what got worse

Claims from the builder's third-pass list, checked in the stills:

- "Hatch lines fade out before they get closer together than a pixel can show. That was the comb on the horizon while the plan opens." Holds in part. Some far strokes are gone in `04-lens3`. The row of boxes at the top right is still there, the paving lines are unchanged, the dotted hairlines in `03-lens2` are unchanged, and the measured flicker did not move (2.4 and 3.0). Whatever blinks in the middle of the opening, this did not reach it.
- "Three room titles had landed on objects (a painting, a pendant, a track light). They went back." True as written, and they went back to their round 1 spots, which were on objects too. `11-living`: the title is still on the framed artwork and the L and I of LIVING sit on its black square, readable now thanks to a pale backing. `18-gallery`: off the track light, back on the blurred first artwork and under the logo. `12-dining`: off the body of the pendant, the G of DINING still touches its rim, and the title is back on top of its own crosshair marker, which pass 2 had cleared.
- "The bedding has more body." Holds for the sheet fold and the top of the throw. Does not hold for the throw's drop, the duvet or the pillows.
- "The lamp shades are linen and no longer light bulbs." The shades have a colour and a gradient now. I cannot see a weave at this size, and they still light nothing.
- "The garden's fire has flames." Holds. It gives no light yet.
- "Glass goes back into the depth pass once both of its sides are real." Holds. Clear views in `22-bedroom`, `26-bath-end` and `20-galleryEnd`, and the gallery wing in `15-patio` still shows its ink lines behind glass.
- "The page's small print steps back on each side only while the ground behind that side is dark." Holds in `30-roll-rest`. Stepping back is not the same as being readable: half a nav in `27-garden`, logo lost in the leaves in `16-olive`, ruler, nav and counter gone in `23-dress`.
- The mirror ("A mirror that worked from everywhere but the stop"). Holds in `24`, `25` and `26`. Coarse, as noted.

Round 2 claims that did not hold then, checked again:

- Bath mirror: holds now.
- "No see-through bed mid-scan": still does not hold. `21-bedIn` is unchanged.
- "Figures fade before each stop": the "21.00" in `25-bath-mid` is gone. "24." and "18.00" in `14-slide` and the chopped "9." in `21-bedIn` are still there.
- "Darker olive bark": still true in `15-patio`, and in `16-olive` the uplight still bleaches the trunk.

- `03-lens2`: the study mullions went from a clean double line to dotted chains.
- `12-dining`: the title is back on its own crosshair marker.
- `16-olive`: the crown has gone close to black. The title reads better on it and the tree looks heavier.
- `26-bath-end` and `22-bedroom`: with clear glass the white specks now read as stars on the dusk sky.

Still broken and not listed above:

- `12-dining`: the branch under the right pendant still burns out to white.
- `16-olive`: the OPEN FILE link still runs into the progress ruler.
- `23-dress`: still a dead frame. The walnut panel keeps its giant grain.
