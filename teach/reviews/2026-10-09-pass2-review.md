# Lintel · Casa Serra. Outside review of pass 2

Date: 9 October 2026
Reviewer: outside creative director, same as round 1
Material: the 35 stills in `teach/shots/2026-10-09-after-pass2/`, set against the round 1 stills and the BEFORE set. I checked each change the builder listed in the stills.

Scoring is unchanged. 8 means a paying client who asked for premium takes that part with no notes. I used quarter points twice, where a part moved but still carries notes. Flicker cannot be judged from stills.

Short version: every item is better again. No item reaches 8. Three of the builder's claims do not hold in the stills (the bath mirror, the see-through bed, plan figures while the camera moves).

---

## 1. Plan lines and the opening

**Round 1: 7. Round 2: 7.25.**

The only change here is the labels, and it is a real one. In `00-hero` PRIVATE GARDEN, BATH, PATIO, KITCHEN AND DINING, ENTRANCE, MEDIA, LIBRARY and MASTER BEDROOM all sit on clear paper with their figures readable. Everything else is as it was. `03-lens2` and `04-lens3` match round 1 almost line for line and the numbers for the middle (2.37 at 120 vh, 3.02 at 170 vh) are unchanged. That stretch is what the client called blinking, so this item is stuck until it is done.

Remaining fixes:

1. Calm 120 to 170 vh. Hairlines on the garage slab and entry path still break into dots in `03-lens2`. Roof edges and glass hatch still pile into a comb on the horizon in `04-lens3`. Minimum line width of about 1.25 px, and fade the paving grid and hatch by distance and angle.
2. `01-scroll1`: the room counter still lands on "8.00" and "3.00". The small logo sits right on the "LINTEL · CASA SERRA" line, so the name reads twice.
3. The garage walls are still uncapped and dotted in `03-lens2`.
4. Small: both GUEST SUITE labels touch their bed symbols.

## 2. The scan follows the camera (patio)

**Round 1: 7.5. Round 2: 7.75.**

Cleaner in every still. `14-slide` has lost the white cut-out trees, the stars in the sky and the neon floor. In `15-patio` the gallery wing shows ink benches and a ring sculpture behind the glass where it was blank white, and no figures are left at the stop. The top fix from round 1 is still open, which is why this is not an 8.

Remaining fixes:

1. Scan by distance. In `14-slide` the loungers and the umbrella are still paper while the lawn, the stone wall and the cypresses behind them are real. The umbrella is a white shape on a stick against the sky.
2. In the same still "24." is still chopped by the dark wall and "18.00" still hangs by the pool. Fade a figure when the camera starts toward it, not only at the stop.
3. `15-patio`: the bronze mullions and concrete band of the gallery wing are finished around paper panels. Let the frame arrive with the room.
4. Nav and room counter nearly vanish on the dark paving in `14-slide`.

## 3. The gallery

**Round 1: 6.5. Round 2: 7.5.**

Both of the client's complaints are answered. The ragged band at the head of the glazing is gone in `19-galleryMid`, and so is the one on the kitchen beam in `12-dining` and `13-turn-to-patio`. In `18-gallery` the second artwork and the bronze spheres are sharp, no orange bars cross finished objects, and "24.00" and "3.60" are gone. What is left is the title and some finish.

Remaining fixes:

1. The title in `18-gallery` moved off the artwork onto the busiest patch of the frame. A black track light sits between THE and GALLERY, orange grid lines run behind the words and an ink edge cuts through "3.00". Park it on calm paper or hold it until the ceiling front has passed.
2. The first artwork on the left is still soft and fills a fifth of the frame. Sharpen it or start the stop a metre further in.
3. The large white dots in `19-galleryMid` and `20-galleryEnd` still read as snow indoors. Smaller, fewer, warmer.
4. In `19-galleryMid` the scan front on the ceiling moves in square blocks and leaves a faint stepped edge.

## 4. The master bedroom

**Round 1: 6.5. Round 2: 7.**

The frame reads properly now. In `22-bedroom` the title is white and legible, "3.60" is gone, the rug has a cream border, the nightstand grain is fine, the floor is paler and the black boards catch a soft graze of light. The soft goods moved less than the list suggests. The pillows are rounder, but the top sheet is still a thin white board, the throw is still a folded card, and side by side with round 1 I can barely tell the lamp shades apart.

Remaining fixes:

1. Bedding. Round the mattress edge, roll the sheet over the duvet, give the throw sag and two or three creases where it drops over the side.
2. Lamps. The shades need a gradient (cream at the rims, warm in the middle) and a pool of light on the leather and the nightstand top. The ceramic bases show their facets.
3. `21-bedIn`: the bed is still see-through mid-scan. Pillows show through the duvet and the bench is still a glossy white capsule. Keep every object opaque, ink on paper or finished.
4. The view through the glass has turned milky and specks still drift in front of the curtain.

## 5. The bath

**Round 1: 7. Round 2: 7.5.**

The room is well composed now. `26-bath-end` is level, the plant stands whole behind the tub, a brass light bar gives the wall glow a source, the vanity has drawers with gaps, pulls and a believable grain, the stool is stone, the mat and the filler have weight. The mirror is not fixed. In `25-bath-mid` and `26-bath-end` it is a blank pale panel, and in `24-bath-start` it is still the blue screen. I stretched the contrast inside it and found no tub, no window and no slat wall.

Remaining fixes:

1. The mirror. It has to show the window bars, the tub and the wall opposite with real contrast, from the first frame of the stop.
2. The red line down the left pier is still in `26-bath-end`. There is also a pink fringe on the right sconce and a ragged right edge on the mirror.
3. The garden through the glass is washed out. The olive is a pale ghost in `26-bath-end`. It was dark and crisp in round 1.
4. `25-bath-mid`: "21.00" hangs in the middle of the window. In `24-bath-start` the plant is half transparent.

## 6. The private garden

**Round 1: 6.5. Round 2: 7.5.**

The biggest jump of the round. `27-garden` finally has one idea: fire in front, then pool, sea and ridge, with the olive closing the right side. The title sits alone in the sky, the stray "21.00", the dashed line and the pink leaf slab are gone, the pool is wide with a pale coping and the lawn is green. It stops short of 8 because the hero of the frame does not glow and the bottom third is heavy.

Remaining fixes:

1. The fire has no fire. It is a bowl of black eggs with a few orange dots. No flame, and no warm light on the rim, the chairs or the grass.
2. The bottom of the frame. Shadows are near black and swallow the scroll hint and the nav (only WORK survives). A stray paving slab is cut off by the frame under the hint.
3. Planting on the right is still toy-like. The lavender reads as clusters of purple balls, the clipped ball is a perfect sphere in camouflage print under the progress ruler, the olive trunk is still pale tan.
4. The sky is a flat beige haze with no dusk colour, and the scrub on the ridge is still evenly spaced blobs.

## 7. The ending

**Round 1: 6.5. Round 2: 7.**

The roll is staged properly now. `30-roll-rest` gives the tube its beat beside the house with a soft shadow on the grass, and in `31-roll-1` ink shows at once (grid bubbles 1 to 6 and the "32.00" dimension). The chrome no longer fights the dark ground, and `34-finale` does carry the button, the SEE ALL WORK link and the outline wordmark. The last look at the built house is still the weak frame, and it picked up a new fault.

Remaining fixes:

1. `29-top-built`. A sawtooth moiré covers the garage floor and the entry path. It is gone one still later, so it will shimmer in motion. Violet and red lines still trace the wall tops, shadows are still near black, the forecourt is still a blank slab, the house is no larger in frame, and a pale strip with a dead straight edge runs down the left of the lawn.
2. The logo turns white for the dark ground and then disappears on the paper in `30-roll-rest`.
3. The tube has no paper edge or thickness where the sheet leaves it, and the print on it is a faint grid, not the plan. Both ends are out of frame, so there is no spiral to see.
4. In `28-lift` the meadow olives are black confetti on bare branches and look burnt. In `34-finale` the headline still crosses the living room walls of the plan.

## Overall

Round 2 cleared most of the system faults from last time. The ragged ceilings are gone, titles are legible on dark, figures no longer sit in finished rooms at the stops, the white cut-out trees are gone and wood grain is in scale on the furniture. The garden and the gallery gained a full point each. What keeps the page under 8 is now short enough to name. The opening still blinks in the middle and was not touched. The bath mirror shows nothing. Bedding and lamps are stiff and flat. The fire does not burn. The top view before the roll is murky and has a moiré. One new habit to watch: several titles were moved clear of their markers and landed on something worse. I would spend the next round on those six things and nothing else.

## Broken or worse than round 1

Claims that did not hold:

- Bath mirror as "a real reflection". Blank panel in `25` and `26`, blue screen in `24`.
- "No see-through bed mid-scan". `21-bedIn` is close to identical to round 1.
- "Figures fade before each stop". True at the stops. Not while moving: `14-slide`, `25-bath-mid`, and a chopped "9." in `21-bedIn`.
- "Darker olive bark". True in `15-patio`. In `16-olive` the uplight still bleaches the trunk.

Worse than round 1:

- `11-living`: the title now sits on the framed artwork. The black square swallows the L of LIVING.
- `12-dining`: the title now lies across the brass pendant. In round 1 it only touched its edge.
- `22-bedroom` and `26-bath-end`: milky view through the glass.
- `29-top-built`: sawtooth moiré and the straight-edged strip on the lawn.
- `30-roll-rest`: white logo on paper.

Still broken and not listed above:

- `12-dining`: the branch under the right pendant still burns out to white.
- `16-olive`: the logo is lost in the leaves. The OPEN FILE link runs into the progress ruler, here and in `22-bedroom`.
- `23-dress`: still a dead frame. The walnut panel on the right keeps its giant grain, and ruler, nav and counter vanish on it.
- `07-hall`: dust reads as snow indoors.
