# Lintel · Casa Serra: whole page rating

13 October 2026. Outside review, blind. I opened only the evidence named in the brief.

## What I looked at and how

I opened all 54 stills: 35 desktop, 9 retina, 10 phone. I went through the 35 desktop stills a second time four to a sheet at 960 px wide each, and looked at four phone stills at native size.

I cut 129 crops. The findings below rest on 52 of them that I studied at full pixel size and took notes on: 38 from the retina stills and 14 from the desktop stills.

From the four recordings I pulled about 920 frames, counting overlaps. About 210 from the two opening clips. About 610 from the desktop walk: overview sheets, fifteen runs of 25 frames, four runs of 12 truly consecutive frames, and 30 full-size frames. About 107 from the phone walk. I also measured the frame-to-frame change across all 3,082 frames of the desktop walk with ffmpeg, so pops were found by number and then checked by eye.

One thing to know before the scores. The recordings (12 Oct) show a "Work in progress" badge beside the top button and a second credit line in the footer. Today's stills show neither. So the clips and the stills are not the same build in every detail. I judged motion from the clips and finish from today's stills.

How each statement is marked:

- **[crop]** checked on a crop at full pixel size
- **[frame]** checked on frames pulled from a recording
- **[measured]** from the frame-to-frame numbers
- **[still]** an impression from the full still, not checked closer

Pixel areas are given in the pixel size of the named file.

## Item notes

### 1. Opening and first impression: 7.5

- Holds: loader lines, bare desk, paper roll and sheet are one idea. The headline is already printed on the paper as it unrolls. [frame]
- Holds: the plan has grid bubbles, dimension strings, wall poche, door swings, a scale bar, a north arrow and a title block. It reads as an architect's sheet at first glance. The scroll hint sits on blank paper and stays quiet. [crop]
- Biggest fault: finish of the sheet at rest. In `desktop/00-hero.jpg` the PRIVATE GARDEN label is struck through by a hatch band (x 1210-1320, y 740-760). The BATH sub-label sits on tile hatch. The small area lines under each room name are soft. A few stray star marks sit round the private garden. [crop]
- Fix: knock the hatch out behind every label. Redraw the small labels as live text or a sharper texture. Clear the stray marks.
- Smaller: the bottom dimension string runs close to the bottom edge of the window. [still] The loader type is small and plain next to the rest of the page, and the desk is an empty brushed surface for about a second. [frame]

### 2. Plan becomes building: 7.5

- Holds: the idea needs no caption. The plan tilts, walls rise with black tops, the camera drops to the door, the door pivots, material floods in. [frame]
- Holds: lines stay crisp and single through the change. No doubled or broken strokes in `retina/06-door.jpg` or in frames 150 to 269. [crop, frame]
- Biggest fault: there is no composed first picture in real material at the door. `desktop/05-approach.jpg` and `desktop/06-door.jpg` are ink only. Material first appears mid-move, in walk frame 452, with the door leaf filling the left third and open sky above a half-flooded hall. [frame]
- Fix: let the door wall flood to stone and timber while the camera rests on the approach. Hold one beat. Then open the door.
- Smaller: `desktop/04-lens3.jpg` has a tangle of wall-top lines in a band behind the facade (y 320-370) and the top third is empty paper. [still] The bench flashes solid orange during the flood. [frame]

### 3. Rooms and gardens as pictures: 7.5

- Holds: living room, olive patio, gallery mid, bedroom, bath and private garden are real pictures with a subject, a foreground and a far view. [still, and the retina set]
- Holds: variety between stops is the strongest thing here. Each room has its own palette. [still]
- Biggest fault: awkward views at or near rests. `desktop/20-galleryEnd.jpg` rests on a blank paper opening that takes about 40% of the frame (x 420-970, y 80-830) with one armchair in it. [still] `desktop/23-dress.jpg` is a wardrobe side, a lone pouf and a blank white wall. [still, crop of the wood]
- Fix: at the gallery end, flood the bedroom earlier or draw the bed into the ink view. In the dressing room, turn the camera to look along the shelves toward the bath window.
- Smaller: `desktop/13-turn-to-patio.jpg` and `desktop/17-to-gallery.jpg` are mostly blank wall if someone stops there. The hall in `desktop/07-hall.jpg` has a blank right wall and a flat rug as its foreground. [still]

### 4. Materials and texture: 7.0

- Holds: stone floors are the best material on the page. Mixed tile sizes, tone variation, pitting, crisp joints. I found no repeats in eight floor crops. [crop]
- Holds: travertine island, brass, the bath tub, the black sculpture, olive bark, leather headboard, bed linen and garden armchair fabric all read correctly at retina size. [crop]
- Biggest fault: plaster walls and ceilings are flat fill. See the left third and ceiling of `retina/12-dining.jpg` and the right wall of `retina/07-hall.jpg` (x 1900-2860, y 950-1550). A smooth gradient, no skirting, no shadow gap, no joint. They also sit a cool lavender-grey beside the warm paper. [crop]
- Fix: a fine plaster grain with slight unevenness, a shadow gap or skirting line at the floor, and a warmer wall white.
- Smaller: the living room timber floor is smeared at retina size (`retina/11-living.jpg`, x 0-960, y 1200-1800). Sofa cushions are smooth foam blocks with no seams. Dining chair backs have a coarse texture that reads as neither wood nor weave. Rugs are flat with no thickness. The wardrobe grain in `desktop/23-dress.jpg` is over-scaled. [crop]

### 5. Light and atmosphere: 7.0

- Holds: one low golden-hour sun runs through the whole walk. Sun patches and mullion shadows on the floors, dappled leaf shadow in the garden, contact shadows under furniture. [crop]
- Holds: the haze over the valley in `desktop/27-garden.jpg` adds depth without flattening the picture. [still]
- Biggest fault: exposure at both ends. At a rest stop the olive trunk and the underside of the canopy are crushed to black in `desktop/16-olive.jpg` (trunk x 680-860, y 180-830). [crop] On the lift the pool carries a burnt white glint about 100 px wide in walk frame 2644. [frame] One brass pendant has a burnt flare in `desktop/12-dining.jpg` near x 1335, y 230. [crop]
- Fix: add a soft fill on the shadow side of the tree, clamp the sun reflection on water, cap the bloom on the pendant.
- Smaller: the view through the kitchen glass is a flat yellow wash. The cypresses in `retina/27-garden.jpg` go to black. [crop]

### 6. Detail and furnishing: 6.5

- Holds: the small things are chosen with care. Tray, books, bowl and branch vase on the coffee table. Lemons on the island. Folded towels. A bath tray with bowl and cup. A print leaning on the headboard shelf. [crop]
- Holds: stools, tub, bed and garden armchairs are well modelled. Window frames have real thickness. [crop]
- Biggest fault: planting and the far landscape read as placeholders. Cypresses are smooth cones with painted streaks (`retina/27-garden.jpg`, x 1900-2300, y 150-750). The hill through the kitchen glass shows triangular facets (`retina/12-dining.jpg`, x 2100-2260, y 560-660). The plant in the bath is flat oval discs. Background trees are dark blobs. [crop]
- Fix: give cypress and background trees the same leaf-card treatment as the olive, smooth the far hills, swap the bath plant for a modelled one.
- Smaller: dining chairs are boxy slabs. The arc lamp arm is visibly faceted. The fireplace is a black box with a glow and nothing inside. Hanging clothes are flat slabs. One gallery pier is still half paper at rest (`retina/18-gallery.jpg`, x 1950-2090, y 530-1170). No skirting, inner doors or switches anywhere. [crop]

### 7. Type, copy and content: 7.0

- Holds: stencil display, mono caps and a plain grotesk make one consistent system. The room label lockup (crosshair, rule, orange room number, title, spec line) is distinctive. [crop]
- Holds: the copy has a voice. "A table for 14." "An olive from c. 1890." "The valley, then the sea." "Then we argue about stone." [still]
- Biggest fault: text collides with the picture. In `desktop/16-olive.jpg` the scroll hint sits on the trunk and the gravel and its first word is lost (x 615-825, y 810-822). In `desktop/20-galleryEnd.jpg` the hint sits on an ink line and reads as struck through. In `desktop/12-dining.jpg` a ceiling edge runs through the spec line. [crop] In `desktop/03-lens2.jpg` the top button covers the LIBRARY label. [still]
- Fix: move the hint out of the picture into the bottom bar beside the room counter. Give spec lines a quiet paper chip behind them.
- Also costing points: content is thin for a studio page. One sentence on who Lintel is, one on the house, nothing on why to get in touch. [still] Spec lines and nav are very small. [crop] Floor labels read upside down on the way into the bedroom and bath (walk frames 2004-2022 and 2290-2313). [frame]

### 8. Ending and call to action: 7.0

- Holds: the roll back to paper is the best single move on the page. The ink plan lands in exact register with the built house and it bookends the opening. [frame, and stills 30 to 33]
- Holds: the closing headline and the line about the practice are good. [still]
- Biggest fault: the last look at the built house sits on a cheap ground. In `desktop/29-top-built.jpg` the field is a blurry mottled green with dot trees and pale specks. In `desktop/28-lift.jpg` the grass is sparse star-shaped sprites on flat green. [crop] It is the weakest surface in the walk and it sits at the climax.
- Fix: a site-plan ground instead of a game lawn. Muted grass with contour lines, a drive, low walls and tree shadows, graded toward the paper colour.
- Smaller: the closing block uses the left third and leaves the rest empty. [still] The button is small for the last ask and there is no contact detail. The footer is one row of four small items with no contact line. [crop]

### 9. Motion: 6.5

- Holds: the route is a logical loop through the house. The rhythm is steady, about three to four seconds of rest and two to three of travel. [measured, frame]
- Holds: labels draw in with their rule. The living room flood is slow enough to read. The roll is smooth. [frame]
- Biggest fault: things pop and text does not leave cleanly. The kitchen island turns solid orange for two frames (walk frames 1082-1083). Sculpture, pot and bench do the same. Dimension numerals stay floating after a room is built: "3.60" is cut to ".60" by a pier and "5.00" hangs over the glass (frames 1076-1100). On the way into the bedroom "3.60" grows very large and is cut by the right edge (frame 2016). [frame]
- Fix: flood objects with an outline only, never a solid fill. Fade every drawing numeral out before the material arrives.
- Smaller: the bedroom reveal lands in about three captured frames with the largest single-frame jump in the clip (frame 2038). [measured, frame] The hall ceiling arrives late, so open sky shows above the walls (frame 452). The rise out of the hall drags pendant globes and a cord across the frame (frame 632). The turn into the gallery fills the frame with a dark mullion for three frames (1649-1651). [frame]

### 10. Phone: 6.5

- Holds: the walk is reframed for portrait. Living room, bedroom, bath and garden work well upright. [still, frame]

In `phone/07-hall.jpg` the crosshair sits on the pendant globes, the title crosses the top edge of the opening, and "Open file" lands on a line of the drawing (x 270-725, y 540-750). ## What I could not judge, and why

- **Today's loader and opening motion.** The opening clips are from 12 Oct. Today's stills only show the finished sheet.
- **True smoothness.** About one frame in five inside every move is an exact repeat of the frame before. That includes the flat closing slide (25%) and the roll (42%), where nothing heavy is drawn. So it points to the recorder, not the page, and I took no points for it. Real frame rate on a real machine is unknown.
- **Shimmer, texture crawl and jagged edges in motion.** Compressed 30 fps frames cannot show them.
- **Scroll feel.** Weight, snapping, what happens on a fast or backward scroll. The clips are a scripted scroll.
- **What "Open file" does.** Every label offers it and no still shows it open. If real project content sits behind it, the content score (7d) would move.
- **Nav targets, hover and focus states.** Work, Studio and Contact are never shown opened.
- **Phone on a real device.** Touch scrolling, address bar resize, speed, heat. Six stops exist on phone only as 390 px frames from the 12 Oct clip.
- **The footer credit line.** It is in the 12 Oct clip and not in today's still. I cannot tell if it was removed or is out of view.
- **Loading time and sound.** Not in the evidence.

## Overall

**7.0.** I would not call the page at the bar of 8 as a whole: the concept, the copy, the roll and six of the room pictures are there, but a paying client who asked for premium would send notes on the text collisions, the outdoor ground and planting, and the thin close.

## The three changes that would raise the page most, in order

1. **One overlay pass, desktop and phone.** Move the scroll hint out of the picture. Back the spec lines. Fade drawing numerals before material arrives. Turn floor labels to face the approach. Dock the phone label. Clear the hatch from behind plan labels. This touches items 1, 3, 7, 9 and 10 and it is the cheapest of the three.
2. **Rebuild the outdoors.** Far hills, cypress and background trees to the olive's standard. A site-plan ground for the top view. Fill light on the olive's shadow side. Clamp the pool glint. Items 5, 6 and 8, and the far plane of every room with glass.
3. **Finish the close and the weak stops.** Give the door a rest in real material. Recompose the gallery end and the dressing room. Then use the empty two thirds of the closing section for who the studio is, two or three proofs and a real contact line, with a footer that carries contact details. Items 2, 3, 7 and 8.
