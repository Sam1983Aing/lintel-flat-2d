## Try it yourself (one per part)

- **How the page works.** Open `section-cut.html` and move the cut to 0.4 m and to 2.4 m. Say what the drawing loses each time.
- **How it got made.** On your next review, give every note a number out of ten and say the number you want.
- **One prompt.** Run the starter prompt on your own subject and stop at the first checkpoint. Look at what you have before you ask for more.
