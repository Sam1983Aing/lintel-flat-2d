# Five thumbnail prompts for the Lintel video

13 October 2026. Each prompt stands alone: paste one into Codex after it has looked at the page. The paths are from `014 Flat 3D/`. Every prompt asks for the page's real look, so the thumbnail matches what people see when they click.

The page's own colours and type, used in all five:

- Paper `#efebe2`, ink `#1d1d1f`, signal orange `#f0500f`
- Headline type: Saira Stencil One, uppercase (the page's own headline font)
- Small labels: Geist Mono, uppercase, wide letter spacing

---

## 1. The split (my pick)

One house, seen from above. The left half is still the drawing, the right half is built.

```text
Make a YouTube thumbnail, 1280 x 720 px, 16:9.

Subject: the house from my landing page (001 Lintel) seen from straight above, an L-shaped courtyard house with a patio, a pool and an olive tree. Use teach/shots/2026-10-09-l4-pass5/00-hero.jpg for the floor plan and 29-top-built.jpg for the built house. They are the same view.

Composition: split the house down the middle with a thin glowing orange line (#f0500f). Left of the line it is the architect's floor plan: black ink (#1d1d1f) on warm paper (#efebe2), thick black walls, room names, dimension lines. Right of the line it is the finished house in late afternoon sun: stone floors, furniture, a dark blue pool, green lawn, long soft shadows. On the right half the walls have real height, so tilt the whole view about 15 degrees to show it is 3D. The paper side stays flat.

Text, top left, in a heavy stencil typeface like Saira Stencil One, uppercase, ink colour: "FLAT PLAN." and under it "REAL HOUSE." with "HOUSE" in orange #f0500f. Under that, a small pill-shaped tag, ink background with paper-coloured mono caps: "BUILT WITH OPUS 5.5".

Rules: the text must read at 320 px wide. No other words, no logos, no watermark, no fake interface. Keep the bottom right corner empty. Nothing important within 40 px of the edges.
```

## 2. Walls rising off the sheet

The most 3D of the five. The plan is a real sheet of paper, and the house is growing out of it.

```text
Make a YouTube thumbnail, 1280 x 720 px, 16:9.

Subject: an architect's floor plan printed on a sheet of warm paper (#efebe2), lying on a pale drafting table, seen from a three-quarter angle like a product photo. The plan is the one from my landing page: use teach/shots/2026-10-09-l4-pass5/03b-walls-rising.jpg for the layout and the ink style (thick black walls, room names like LIVING, PATIO, KITCHEN AND DINING, dimension lines).

The trick: the walls are rising out of the drawing as real 3D walls, about a third of their height. The front left part of the house is still flat ink on the paper. Toward the back right it becomes a real miniature house: plaster walls, a stone patio, a small olive tree, a dark blue pool, tiny furniture, warm late afternoon light with long shadows falling across the paper. One thin orange line (#f0500f) crosses the sheet where drawing turns into building.

Text, upper left, heavy stencil typeface like Saira Stencil One, uppercase, ink colour (#1d1d1f): "SCROLL TO" and under it "BUILD IT" with "BUILD" in orange. Bottom left, a small tag in mono caps, paper text on an ink pill: "OPUS 5.5".

Style: a clean 3D render, shallow depth of field, soft studio light, a little paper grain. The text must read at 320 px wide. No other words, no logos, no watermark. Keep the bottom right corner empty.
```

```text
Make a YouTube thumbnail, 1280 x 720 px, 16:9.

Subject: the living room from my landing page at eye level. Use teach/shots/2026-10-09-l4-pass5/11-living.jpg as the reference: a sunken sofa in warm beige, a board-marked concrete fireplace with a fire, an arc lamp with a brass shade, a potted olive tree, late afternoon sun on the floor.

The trick: the left two thirds of the picture are fully real, with warm materials and light. Through the wide opening on the right, the next room (a kitchen with bar stools) is still only a black line drawing on white paper, as in the reference. Make that contrast strong and obvious: colour and light on one side, clean ink lines on the other, with a thin orange edge (#f0500f) where they meet.

Text, top left, over the ceiling, heavy stencil typeface like Saira Stencil One, uppercase, paper colour (#efebe2) with a soft dark shadow so it reads: "NO MAGIC" and under it "PROMPT" in orange. Top right, a small tag in mono caps, ink text on a paper pill: "OPUS 5.5".
```

```text

Text, on the left third, heavy stencil typeface like Saira Stencil One, uppercase, ink colour (#1d1d1f), very large: "2D" then a short orange arrow pointing right, then "3D" in orange. Under it, a small tag in mono caps, paper text on an ink pill: "WITH OPUS 5.5".
```

## Notes

- **Why 1 is my pick.** It is the same picture as the link preview on the live page (`tools/publish/media/share-split.jpg`), so the post, the link and the video look like one thing. And it can be made from the two real screenshots, so it can't drift from the page.
- **Words.** Each one has a different hook, so you can also mix: any headline works on any picture. "NO MAGIC PROMPT" is the one that answers the comments.
- **Image models and text.** They often misspell. If the letters come out wrong, generate the picture with no text and set the words yourself in Saira Stencil One.
- **Your face.** None of these has it. If you want it, ask for the same picture with the right third left empty and add your cut-out there.
- **No logos.** "OPUS 5.5" as plain text is fine. I left the Claude and Anthropic logos out on purpose.
