## Sam's direction for 007 to 009 (30 Sept, evening)

paint filter, which is what reads as "weird, no detail"), and a real product
landing page with one goal the scroll leads to. Three style and product pairs

cut tent.

## Sam's direction for 004 to 006
Same structure as The Long Second: a telephoto plate that reads as a flat
picture, a dolly zoom that opens it, then a long flight deep along Z through
layers, four stops with labels painted into the scene, a blank-beat
interstitial, and a return to the flat plate. Floating dust for depth. The
scroll is the story.

The look must be "more realistic but artistic": real scanned materials and
real skies, not textures painted in code. The picture quality comes from
light (low sun, lamps, shafts, haze), a filmic grade and a light paint filter
that is strongest on the flat plate and fades as you go in.

## Shared engine
`shared/ENGINE.md` documents it. `fx-core.js` (geometry, loader, materials),
`fx-post.js` (photographic finisher), `fx-paint.js` (paint finisher with depth
of field, light shafts and a woodblock print mode, dust and rain streaks drawn
after the paint), `fx-experience.js` (scroll film, uploads every texture at
load so nothing stalls mid-scroll), `fx-ui.css` (HUD), `fx-ink.js` (clean
finisher for 007 to 009: ink lines from depth and creases, flat toon colour,
pen hatching, no paint filter).
